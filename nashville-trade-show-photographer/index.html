For show organizers

Your recap is your sales deck for next year. We cover the expo floor at peak traffic, every sponsor activation, the awards and the networking events, and we cut a highlight reel and sponsor-specific clips that make renewals easy. The TWSRA reel ends on a direct ask: sponsor, exhibit, attend.

For exhibitors

Book us for your booth alone. We photograph your booth clean before doors open, then busy with buyers, capture product shots on the stand, record short interviews with your team and customers, and deliver vertical clips you can post during the show while buyers are still on the floor.

How we work a show floor

Expo halls are dark, loud and crowded. We shoot fast cinema cameras at high frame rates for slow motion, pull elevated wides when the venue allows, and grade so products and faces read correctly even under warehouse lighting. We follow the show's exhibitor-appointed contractor rules and badge in like any other contractor.

Design for the booth

Our design studio builds booth graphics, one-sheets, pamphlets, product cards and social promotion for the show, so the photos and the booth look like the same brand.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are you booking 2027 events?

Yes. We are booking trade show coverage for 2027 now, including events already on Music City Center's 2027 calendar. Send your dates and we will hold them.

Do you photograph trade shows at Music City Center?

Yes. We cover trade shows and expos at Music City Center and other Nashville venues, for organizers and for individual exhibitors.

Can we book you for just our booth?

Yes. Exhibitors book us for booth photos, product shots, interviews and social clips during the show.

Do you follow exhibitor-appointed contractor rules?

Yes. We register through the show as an exhibitor-appointed contractor when required, follow the show's badge and move-in rules, and coordinate with the general contractor.

Can you post content during the show?

Yes. We deliver edited photos and vertical clips during the show so your team can post while buyers are on the floor.

What trade shows have you covered?

Recent work includes the 2026 Tennessee Wine & Spirits Retailers Association trade show in Nashville: the expo floor, exhibitor booths, awards ceremony and step-and-repeat.

How do we book?

Email us or call (773) 573-2135 with the show name, dates and your booth number or the areas you need covered.
