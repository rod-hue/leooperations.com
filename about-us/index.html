About Leo Operations

Our story

Leo Operations began with a simple idea: the people who knock the doors should also be the people who tell the story. Our founder organized his first campaigns as a student at Tennessee State University, interned in the Tennessee Senate, and went on to lead field programs and campaigns across 25 states, including the 2020 Bloomberg presidential campaign in Tennessee.

Today our team runs campaign strategy, field programs, video and photo production, websites and social media for mayors, members of Congress, judges, legislators, unions, nonprofits and brands. We are proudly Black-owned and headquartered in Nashville.

How we work

  • Strategy first: every program starts with the win number.
  • One team: field, film, web and social under one roof.
  • Receipts: we report the doors, the views and the results.
