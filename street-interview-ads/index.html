FAQ

Before you book.

What happens on the strategy call?

It is 30 minutes, one on one. We look at the ads you are running now, map the first 10 street polls we would film for your brand, and walk you through the three-month program. If we are not a fit, we will tell you.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Yes. Every partnership starts with a three-month minimum. It takes three months to test, find the winners and scale them.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many polls you need each month and whether we run the paid media too. You get exact pricing for your brand on the call.

Are the people in the videos real?

Yes. Real people and genuine reactions. Everyone who appears signs a release before their clip is used.

Can we use the videos for paid ads and organic?

Both. Clips are built to work organically first, then scaled through paid media once they prove themselves.

Where do you film?

We are based in Nashville and travel nationally. We film in the cities and neighborhoods where your customers are.
