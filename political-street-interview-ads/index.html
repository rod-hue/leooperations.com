FAQ

Before you book.

What happens on the strategy call?

It is 30 minutes, one on one. We look at the ads you are running now, map the first 10 street polls we would film for your race, and walk you through the three-month program. If we are not a fit, we will tell you.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Yes. Every program starts with a three-month minimum, because it takes that long to film, test and scale the clips that work. Start at least three months before early voting opens.

What does it cost?

It depends on how many polls you need each month, how many markets we film in and whether we also run the paid media. You get exact pricing for your race on the call.

Are the people in the videos real?

Yes. Real voters in their own words. Everyone who appears signs a release, and we never present an actor as a voter.

Can political street interview ads run on TikTok?

Not as paid ads. TikTok does not allow paid political ads, so campaigns post clips there as organic content. Paid political ads run on Meta, YouTube and Google after advertiser verification, with "paid for by" disclaimers.

Do you offer free interviews for candidates?

For state and local candidates, we can film a short on-camera interview at no cost where state campaign finance law allows it. A company cannot give free services to a federal candidate, so for Senate, congressional and presidential campaigns the interview is part of a paid program.

Where do you film?

We are based in Nashville and travel nationally. Our team has worked on campaigns in 25 states, and we film where your voters actually are.
