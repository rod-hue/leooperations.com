Where we work

Nashville roots.National reach.

Leo Operations is a Black-owned campaign, media and marketing firm based in Nashville. Our team has run field and petition programs in more than ten states and films and photographs candidates wherever the race is.

On-cycle and off-cycle

Campaign work never really stops. Off-cycle years like 2027 bring governor's races in Kentucky, Louisiana and Mississippi, the entire Virginia and New Jersey legislatures, and mayor's races in Chicago, Philadelphia, Jacksonville, Nashville and other big cities. On-cycle years like 2028 bring the presidential primaries, 34 U.S. Senate races and all 435 House seats.
