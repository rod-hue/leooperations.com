Who we photograph

Executives, staff teams, candidates for office, elected officials, and pastors and ministry leaders. Recent sessions include South Carolina legislators and local officials, and campaign portraits for U.S. Senate candidate Brandon Brown.

On location

We set up at your office, school, church or a meaningful place in your community, so your headshot reflects where you work and who you serve. For teams, we keep lighting and framing consistent so every photo matches on your website.

Retouched and delivered online

You choose your favorites from an online gallery. We retouch, color correct and deliver high resolution files for print and web, plus crops for LinkedIn and social profiles.

A Black-owned Nashville team

Leo Operations is Black-owned and based in Nashville. We light and edit every skin tone with care.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can you photograph our whole team at our office?

Yes. We set up on location and keep lighting consistent so every team photo matches.

How do we choose our photos?

You get a private online gallery to pick your favorites, then we retouch and deliver the final files.

Do you photograph candidates and elected officials?

Yes. We photograph candidates, legislators and local officials for campaigns, offices and constituent outreach.

Do you travel?

Yes. We are based in Nashville and photograph clients across the South and beyond.
