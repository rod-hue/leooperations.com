Nashville videographer

Nashvillevideographer. Cinematic, on time.

Leo Operations is a Black-owned video production team in Nashville. We film galas, concerts, campaign ads, brand films and weddings in 4K, with drone and a crew that already works together.

What we film

Event coverage, commercials, campaign ads and endorsements, documentaries, social reels, music videos and wedding films. Our work includes DJ AOne's 40th at Madison Square Garden, TSU Homecoming 2025 with Jeezy, Psiryn and Kandi Burruss at Soul Nashville, and a Sierra Club documentary in Phoenix.

How we shoot

Cinema cameras in 4K, clean audio, drone when the location allows, and an editor who cuts for the platform: a 30-second spot, a vertical reel or a full event film. We grade for skin tones of every shade, not just the brightest one in the frame.

Black-owned and Nashville based

We are a Black-owned team based in Nashville, so we know the venues, the HBCU calendar and the community events. We also travel for national work.
