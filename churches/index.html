10 / FAQ

Church questions, answered.

What does Leo Operations do for churches?

Photography, video, sermon clips, websites, social media and graphic design for churches and ministries. One team handles your Sunday coverage, your anniversary film, your website and your weekly posts.

Which churches have you worked with?

Cathedral of Praise, First Baptist Church Capitol Hill, St. Andrew’s Presbyterian, Allen Chapel AME, Mount Bethlehem Baptist, Friendship Missionary Baptist, the Nashville City Missionary Baptist District Association and the Interdenominational Ministers Fellowship, among others.

Can you build our church website?

Yes. We build modern church sites with service times, sermons, events, ministries, plan-your-visit pages and online giving, like the new site we built for St. Andrew’s Presbyterian Church in North Nashville.

Can you turn our sermons into social clips?

Yes. We cut short vertical clips with captions from each sermon so your message reaches people who were not in the room on Sunday.

Do you cover pastoral and church anniversaries?

Yes. We photograph and film anniversaries, revivals and special services, design the programs and save the dates, and deliver recap films your members will share.

Where do you work?

Leo Operations is based in Nashville and works with churches across Tennessee, the South and the Midwest. Our photo, film and design team travels to you.

How do we get started?

Email us about your church, your ministries and what you need. We start with a conversation and follow up with a written proposal.
