Pastor Jerry L. Maynard II

Pastor Jerry L. Maynard II leads Cathedral of Praise in Nashville, runs The Maynard Group and served eight years as an at-large member of the Metro Council. Leo Operations has worked with him since his 2022 campaign and remains a partner across his church, business and community work.

About Pastor Maynard

Pastor

Pastor of Cathedral of Praise, a multicampus church in North Nashville that began in 1966 and, according to his site, has more than 3,000 active members and has given more than $150,000 in scholarships over 20 years.

Entrepreneur

Founder and president of The Maynard Group, a Nashville government relations and consulting firm he started in 2007, and a co-founder of The Nashville Voice.

Civic leader

Elected to the Metro Council at-large in 2007 and re-elected in 2011, serving until 2015. Earlier he was the first African American deputy chairman of the Tennessee Democratic Party.

Community

His site credits him with work to support Nashville General Hospital, the National Museum of African American Music and the Jefferson Street “Gateway to Heritage” project, and with a scholarship fund for Tennessee State University students.
