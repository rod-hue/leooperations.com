Criminal Court Clerk of Nashville, TN – Howard Gentry

Howard Gentry has served as Davidson County Criminal Court Clerk since 2011 and was earlier Nashville’s first African American vice mayor. He won his 2018 primary with about 82% of the vote. Leo Operations’ founder has worked with Clerk Gentry since 2012.

About Howard Gentry

  • Howard Gentry was appointed Davidson County Criminal Court Clerk in 2011 and was re-elected without opposition in 2012. Source
  • Before becoming clerk, he was elected as a Metro Council member-at-large and later as vice mayor, winning re-election as vice mayor in 2003. Source
  • Gentry ran for mayor of Nashville in 2007 and missed the runoff by fewer than 300 votes. Source
  • A Nashville native, he earned a bachelor's degree and a master's degree in education from Tennessee State University. Source
