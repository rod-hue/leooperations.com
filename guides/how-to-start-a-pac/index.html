How to start a PAC: registration, contribution limits, reporting and what it really costs

A practical walkthrough for anyone forming a political action committee, from choosing the PAC type and treasurer to filing Form 1, raising money legally and spending it well.

By Rodriquez Wright II, Founder, Leo Operations · Updated November 2026 · 11 min read

To start a federal PAC, you pick a PAC type, appoint a treasurer, get an EIN, open a dedicated bank account and file FEC Form 1 (Statement of Organization). A nonconnected PAC must register within 10 days of raising or spending more than $1,000 in a calendar year, and a corporate or union PAC must register within 10 days of its establishment, regardless of size. Form 1 carries no filing fee, so registration itself costs $0. The real costs are the treasurer's time, compliance software and the discipline to file every report on schedule.

What is a PAC, and which type should you start?

"PAC" is shorthand for a political committee that is not a candidate's campaign or a party. Under federal law there are two families: separate segregated funds (SSFs), which a corporation, union, trade association or membership group sets up, and nonconnected committees, which anyone else can start. Within the nonconnected family, the choice that matters most is whether you want to give money to candidates, spend independently, or both.

A business or union whose employees want to pool money for candidates should form an SSF, since the sponsor can pay the overhead. A nonconnected PAC must pay its own administration and solicitation costs, which changes the fundraising math. A group that plans to run its own voter contact program at scale usually wants super PAC or hybrid status, and our guide to super PACs and independent expenditures covers that side in depth.

Pick the structure before the first dollar moves. Changing it later means amended filings and possibly refunds.

Federal PAC or state PAC: which rules apply?

The test is where the money goes. A committee that supports or opposes candidates for Congress, the Senate or the presidency answers to the Federal Election Commission. A committee established exclusively for state and local activity does not register with the FEC, though it can trigger federal registration if it starts spending in connection with a federal election.

State PACs register with the state ethics commission, secretary of state or campaign finance board, and every state writes its own thresholds, limits and deadlines. Groups active at both levels run a federal PAC and a separate state committee, each with its own bank account and reports.

How to start a PAC, step by step

This is the federal sequence for a nonconnected PAC. SSFs follow the same steps, with the sponsoring organization named as the connected organization on Form 1.

  1. Write a one-page plan: which races, which type of spending, how much you expect to raise.
  2. Choose the PAC type based on that plan.
  3. Pick a compliant name.
  4. Appoint a treasurer and an assistant treasurer.
  5. Get an EIN from the IRS.
  6. Open a bank account in the PAC's name (two if you are forming a hybrid).
  7. File FEC Form 1 within the 10-day window.
  8. Set up recordkeeping and compliance software before you solicit anyone.

Choosing a PAC name

A nonconnected PAC may not use a federal candidate's name in its official name. Only a candidate's own authorized committee can do that. Pick something short that donors will recognize on a card statement.

Choosing a treasurer

Every PAC needs a treasurer, and the treasurer carries legal responsibility for the committee's filings. FEC guidance lists the duties: making "best efforts" to obtain and report donor information, monitoring contributions for limits and prohibitions, and keeping records of receipts and disbursements for three years. The FEC also urges political committees to name an assistant treasurer, since only a registered assistant treasurer may sign reports when the treasurer is absent. Do it.

A professional compliance treasurer is the standard for any PAC planning to raise serious money, and the FEC's administrative fines for late or missing reports scale with the committee's financial activity and its record of prior violations.

EIN and bank account

The IRS issues an Employer Identification Number online at no charge, and the bank will ask for it. Open the account in the PAC's exact registered name. Never run PAC money through a personal account, even for a week.

Filing FEC Form 1

Form 1 lists the committee's name and address, the treasurer, the bank (the designated depository) and any connected organization. File it within 10 days of the trigger. Committees that raise or spend more than $50,000 in a calendar year, or expect to, must file electronically, so most serious PACs file electronically from day one. Once registered, your committee appears in the FEC's public committee search, which is also how you check whether a name is already taken.

PAC contribution limits for 2025 to 2026

These are the federal limits from the FEC's 2025 to 2026 contribution limits chart. Super PACs and the non-contribution account of a hybrid PAC are exempt from the limits on what they receive.

Who givesTo a candidateTo a PACTo a national party (main account)
Individual$3,500 per election$5,000 per year$44,300 per year
Multicandidate PAC$5,000 per election$5,000 per year$15,000 per year
Non-multicandidate PAC$3,500 per election$5,000 per year$44,300 per year

The $5,000 per election limit is the reason PACs care about multicandidate status. A PAC qualifies once it has been registered at least six months, has received contributions from at least 51 persons, and has contributed to at least five federal candidates. It then files Form 1M within 10 days of meeting those criteria. Until then, your PAC gives at the individual rate.

The FEC indexes some limits for inflation in odd-numbered years only, so expect new indexed figures for the 2027 to 2028 cycle early in 2027. The $5,000 limits for multicandidate PACs and for gifts to PACs are not indexed.

Money a PAC cannot take

  • Cash over $100 from any one source; larger gifts must come by check or other written instrument.
  • Anonymous cash over $50, which must be promptly disposed of.
  • Contributions from foreign nationals, federal contractors, national banks or federally chartered corporations, including to super PACs.
  • Corporate or union treasury money in a traditional PAC's contribution account.

What a PAC files after it registers

Registration starts a reporting obligation that runs until the committee formally terminates. Federal PACs file Form 3X on either a monthly or a quarterly schedule, and a committee may change its filing frequency no more than once per calendar year.

Quarterly filers have extra work in election years. They file pre-election reports if contributions are made within certain time frames before primaries, specials and the general. For that reason, many PACs active in several states choose the monthly schedule, which removes the need to file pre-primary reports state by state.

Two deadlines are live as of this writing in November 2026:

  • The 30-day Post-General report is due December 3, 2026, covering activity through November 23, and the FEC says all registered PACs must file it, even with little or no activity.
  • The Year-End report for quarterly filers is due January 31, 2027, covering November 24 through December 31, 2026.

A PAC registered this month is already inside that cycle. Super PACs and any PAC making independent expenditures also face 24-hour and 48-hour reports; see our guide to super PACs and independent expenditures.

Records you must keep for every dollar

FEC rules require the date and amount for every receipt, name and address for anything over $50, and occupation and employer for anything over $200, with records kept for three years. Build the donation form so it requires employer and occupation fields from the first day.

How to start a PAC in Texas and other states

State PACs follow the same logic with different paperwork. In Texas, a general-purpose committee files Form GTA, the Appointment of a Campaign Treasurer by a General-Purpose Committee, with the Texas Ethics Commission. The commission's 2026 campaign finance guide for political committees sets a $1,110 threshold: a committee may not accept more than $1,110 in political contributions before that appointment is on file, so in practice you file the GTA first and raise money second. The commission adjusts that figure, so check the current year's guide.

Every state has its own version, and some cities add local filings. Before you raise a dollar, pull the current committee guide from your state agency and note three numbers: the registration trigger, the contribution limits and the first report date.

State and local PACs also have an IRS question that federal PACs do not. PACs registered with the FEC are exempt from filing Form 8871, Form 8872 and Form 990. A state or local PAC that expects $25,000 or more in annual receipts generally must file Form 8871 electronically within 24 hours of organization.

Mistakes that sink new PACs

  • Raising money before the bank account exists, then depositing checks into someone's personal account.
  • Treating the treasurer as a title rather than a job, then missing a report in a quiet month.
  • Coordinating with a candidate's campaign on independent spending.
  • Forming the PAC in September of an election year. Multicandidate status alone takes six months.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start a political action committee?

Choose a PAC type, appoint a treasurer, get an EIN, open a bank account in the PAC's name and file FEC Form 1. A nonconnected federal PAC must register within 10 days of raising or spending more than $1,000 in a calendar year. State-only PACs register with their state agency instead.

Is there a PAC registration fee?

There is no fee to file FEC Form 1, so federal registration costs nothing. Check your state agency's committee guide for any state-level charges.

How much does it cost to start a PAC?

Registration is free, and the IRS issues EINs free. A small PAC should budget for compliance software (one vendor lists pricing from $1,495 per year plus setup), treasurer services and a legal review of its structure. Everything above that is program spending, which is the reason the PAC exists.

How do I start a PAC in Texas?

File Form GTA with the Texas Ethics Commission to appoint a campaign treasurer for a general-purpose committee. For 2026, Texas bars a committee from accepting more than $1,110 in political contributions without that appointment on file.

Can one person start a PAC?

Yes. An individual can form a nonconnected PAC, serve as or appoint the treasurer and file Form 1. To give candidates the higher $5,000 per election amount, though, the PAC needs multicandidate status, which requires contributions from at least 51 persons, six months of registration and contributions to at least five federal candidates.

How much can a PAC give to a candidate?

For 2025 to 2026, a multicandidate PAC could give $5,000 per election to a federal candidate, and a non-multicandidate PAC could give $3,500 per election. Primary and general elections count separately. Super PACs cannot contribute to candidates at all.

What is the difference between a PAC and a super PAC?

A traditional PAC takes limited contributions and can give directly to candidates. A super PAC can take unlimited contributions from individuals, corporations and unions, but it may only spend independently and cannot coordinate with candidates. A hybrid PAC runs both models through two separate bank accounts.

Does a PAC have to file with the IRS?

Federal PACs registered with the FEC are exempt from IRS Forms 8871, 8872 and 990. State and local PACs that expect $25,000 or more in annual receipts generally must file Form 8871 within 24 hours of organizing.

How do I look up a PAC registration?

Federal PAC registrations and reports are public on the FEC's committee search at fec.gov. Search by name to see its treasurer and every report it has filed. State PACs appear in each state's campaign finance database.
