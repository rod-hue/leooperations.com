Who you hire

Leo Operations is led by founder Rodriquez Wright II, a Tennessee State University graduate who started at the Tennessee State Capitol in 2012. He was Tennessee organizing director for Michael Bloomberg's 2020 presidential campaign, managed District Attorney Glenn Funk's 2022 re-election in Nashville, and has directed field programs in 25 states.

What a Black political strategist adds

Campaigns win when they reach every neighborhood, not just the ones that vote every year. Our team has knocked more than 650,000 doors, run voter registration on HBCU campuses and in churches, and built media that speaks to Black voters without talking down to them.

Who we work with

Clients include Nashville Mayor Freddie O'Connell, Rep. Justin Jones, Sen. London Lamar, Councilmembers Zulfat Suara and Olivia Hill, Judge Ana Escobar, SEIU Virginia and the National Black Caucus of State Legislators.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Leo Operations a Black-owned political consulting firm?

Yes. Leo Operations was founded in Nashville by Rodriquez Wright II and is Black-owned.

Do you work with candidates outside Tennessee?

Yes. Our team has run campaign, ballot and organizing work in 25 states, from New York and Georgia to Texas, Arizona and California.

What size races do you take on?

Everything from school board and Metro Council to judicial, legislative, statewide and federal races, plus ballot measures and issue campaigns.

How do we start?

Email us your race, district and timeline. We reply within one business day with how we would approach it.
