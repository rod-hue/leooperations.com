07 / FAQ

Wedding questions, answered.

Do you offer wedding photography and videography together?

Yes. Two camera professionals provide coordinated photography and 4K videography, and a third can be added based on your timeline. One team plans and shoots your day, so the photos and the film match.

Where do you photograph and film weddings?

We are based in Nashville and cover weddings across Middle Tennessee, including Brentwood, Franklin and Murfreesboro. We also travel for weddings in other states.

How long is wedding coverage?

Full-day coverage reserves our team for up to eight consecutive hours. We agree on final coverage times with you before booking.

When will we receive our wedding photos and video?

Edited photographs are delivered within three business days of the wedding. Your 5 to 7 minute highlight video is delivered within 14 business days.

Do you film weddings with a drone?

Yes. We plan exterior drone shots of your venue. If rain, high winds or other weather prevents flying, we capture exterior shots from the ground instead.

What do we receive?

A curated, high-resolution photo gallery with color correction and web-ready copies in a downloadable digital gallery, plus a highlight film with color grading, audio enhancement and licensed music.

How do we book?

Email your date, venue and guest count. Once we confirm availability, you receive a written proposal. Your date is reserved when the proposal is signed and the down payment is received.
