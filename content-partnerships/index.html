08 / FAQ

Partnership questions, answered.

What is a content partnership?

A monthly engagement where Leo Operations plans, shoots, designs and delivers your photo, video and graphics on a set schedule, so your brand shows up consistently across every platform.

Can I book just one month?

Yes. A one-month brand sprint works well for a launch, a relaunch, a campaign push or building a personal brand from scratch. Three-month and six-month partnerships build lasting momentum.

What do I receive each month?

A combination of shoot days for photo and video, edited short-form and long-form video, a photo library and graphic design assets. Your proposal spells out the volume for your term.

Do you work with candidates and PACs?

Yes. Political media is where Leo Operations started. We produce content for candidates, elected officials, PACs and advocacy groups year-round, not only in campaign season.

Do your fees include ad spend?

No. Advertising and platform costs are paid directly by the client. We offer ad strategy and management as an additional service.

Do you travel?

Yes. Partnerships include travel within 100 miles of Nashville, and we travel nationwide for partners outside that range.

How many partners do you take on?

A limited number at a time, so every partner gets our full attention and a consistent team.

How do we get started?

Email us with your name and brand, your industry and location, the term you are considering, when you want to start and why now. We reply within one business day to schedule a discovery call.

Payment, scope, travel and usage are covered in our terms of service.
